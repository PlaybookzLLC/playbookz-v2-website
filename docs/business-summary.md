# Business Summary

Playbookz is a personal branding agency focusing on LinkedIn. We focus on the SMB portion of the market.

This is a downstream pivot from what our brand has historically been. Historically, we were a LinkedIn personal branding agency that sold to larger SMBs and mid-market B2B companies at a price point of $3,000-$6,000. We are pivoting to lower price point and more productized services at price points of $997 (Tier 1) and $1,997 (Tier 2).

## Packages

### Tier 1: $999/mo

- Profile optimization & images
- 1:1 Dedicated editor & account manager
- Async communication
- 3 posts per week
- Detailed onboard & deep research into the client's business
- Custom dashboard
- Private Slack channel
- Based on proprietary viral post structures

### Tier 2: $1,999/mo

- Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Custom images for posts
- Paid amplification of posts using our own funds
- Automated outbound (connections + first messages)
- Lead identification

## Guarantees

- No guarantees for Tier 1 (although we would not say that)
- On Tier 2: guarantee 20k reach in the first 30 days of posting or your money back, and 100k reach in the first 6 months or we'll work for free until we hit it

## Model

We are loosely emulating what DesignPickle did for design, but trying to do it for personal branding on LinkedIn.

- Scale your personal brand on autopilot

- 3.2 million views & 6,000+ leads in the last 12 months
